Most of the write-ups about WebSocket auctions skip straight to "and then bids update
live." The part worth writing about is everything before that: reconnection state,
what happens to a bid that lands mid-disconnect, and how you tell a bidder their
offer did or didn't land without lying to them.

Stock Swap's bidding flow leans on a simple rule: the server is the only source of
truth for the current price, and the client never optimistically renders a bid as
accepted. It's a small decision that removes a whole category of bugs.
